# Spysweeper Rules v2

## Objective 
Root out all spies before your opponent

## Deck
Each player brings a deck of 27 cards, with no more than 4 cards having the same number or effect, and no more than 9 elites. Ideally, each player's decks should be sleeved differently

## Setup
Each player draws 4 cards from each deck. If there aren't 4 unique cards or the player wishes for a new hand, a player can reveal the 8 cards to their opponent, place those cards on the bottoms of the respective decks, and draw again (this may be done as many times as wanted).

Player going first places 4 cards with different values in the 2x2 "spy grid", then the player going second follows. The top row are your "spies" and the bottom row are their corresponding "helper", with the values on the cards corresponding to their locations on a shared 3x3 grid. When a spy's square is queried, they must tell a lie. The nature of the "trap" square depends on the spy above it. (It is recommended for both players to use pen and paper to track their own spies/traps, as well as discovered clues).

Shuffle all remaining cards into their respective decks. Draw 2 cards from each deck into the "action space".

## Play
Players take turn doing one of two actions:
    
### Query
Player takes a card from the "action space" and places it in an empty space in the 3x3 grid. Immediately draw a card from the same deck as the taken card into the "action space". Resolve the effect of the query if applicable. Finally, the opponent answers the query based on their "spy grid".

### Accuse
Turn player selects a number (which is not already revealed in the opposing player's "spy grid"). If number is among the opposing player's "spy grid", that player must reveal it. If a "helper" was revealed, the nature of "helper" must also be revealed (spy, liar, both, or neither). If spy was accused, turn player goes again. Otherwise, turn player must reveal a card in their spy grid.
